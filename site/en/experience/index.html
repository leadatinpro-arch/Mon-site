Why it matters: a competitive sector where digital presence must be optimised to stand out. A creative and strategic role: producing visual and written content for social media. Direct impact: every post influences brand awareness and lead generation.

Specialisation in Marketing & Product Innovation.

Saint-Gobain PAM specialises in ductile iron pipe solutions for water transport. A subsidiary of the Saint-Gobain group, it is a world leader in its field with an international reach. The PAM Line project is a strategic redesign of the multilingual website for 12 countries, aiming to modernise the brand image, harmonise digital communication and improve the user experience.

Why it matters: an ageing website (the old site ran on an outdated, hard-to-use Drupal 7), a technical emergency (hosting expiring in November 2025, making the move to Drupal 10 essential), and a strategic stake (improving international visibility, centralising content, strengthening brand consistency).

Key responsibilities
  • International coordination: the link between countries, the central team and developers
  • Running workshops: needs analysis, setting priorities
  • Training local teams: online sessions, how-to guides, ongoing support
  • Managing content through the back office, contributing to two other sites
  • Technical follow-up: reporting bugs via tickets, validating UI components
  • Planning and reporting: tracking deadlines, updating schedules, weekly reporting
